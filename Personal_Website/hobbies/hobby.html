I have been learning Spanish my whole life. My mother's side is Mexican-American but my grandmother decided to not teach her children Spanish. My mother never learned much more than the basics so she didn't teach us either. However, the rest of my family speaks Spanish and that was the language we would use when all of us were together. So I grew up understanding the gist of the language without knowing the actual words. I've been using language learning apps like Duolingo to formally learn Spanish for over a decade. I've made slow progress due to the language part of my brain being damaged by parasites, but I am getting better. My biggest disappointment is that Duolingo doesn't have different dialecs of Spanish so my accent and some of the words I've learned don't match up with my family's.
