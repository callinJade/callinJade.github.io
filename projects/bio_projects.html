My current research is on the evolutionary history of mimosine production in the mimosoid clade of plants. I work under Dr. Donovan Bailey in the Bailey Lab at NMSU. I have been working on this project since I transferred to the university in Fall 2024. Pictured to the left is one of the plants I work with. The objective of this project is to explore the evolutionary history of the genes involved in mimosine production and degradation by collecting relevant genomes, determining orthologous groups, and analysing the evolutionary history for the species and gene trees.
